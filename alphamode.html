Alpha Mode Activated

Status: Upcoming

“Brainrot” culture is not just a meme—it’s a movement. It represents the chaotic, the overstimulated, the absurd edge of internet culture that refuses to be tamed. Embracing the scroll-induced numbness of modern life, this drop mirrors the visual clutter and mental overload with loud designs and glitchy visuals. It's ironic, humorous, and defiant.

The Alpha Mode Activated collection channels that chaotic brilliance into high-contrast streetwear: oversized cuts, distorted graphics, and raw digital noise printed into every piece. This isn’t just fashion—it’s satire, commentary, and rebellion wrapped in threads.

Prepare to switch on your alpha instincts and rot responsibly.
